# Open Promises, People and the Prep Brief (Plan C) Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Say who each 1:1 was with, keep every open promise across meetings on one page, and give each person a page that prepares the next 1:1 (L5).

**Architecture:** Builds on Plan B's records (`action_item` with status, owner speaker ids) and its 1:1 speaker rows. "Who it was with" links the meeting's remote speaker to a `person`; the transcript, quotes and owners then show that name. The Promises page lists open action items across meetings by direction. A person's page shows open promises both ways, what was left for next time and still-open questions from their last 1:1, and an optional brief (A13) written from their recent notes, cited to the meetings and checked like every other point.

**Tech Stack:** as Plans A and B.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-26-ask-notes-promises-design.md` (§3.8, §4.8, §5, §6). BUILD.md §6.5 (consent: names only for people the owner names), §8.2, §8.4, DESIGN.md.

## Global Constraints

- Everything in Plan A's and Plan B's Global Constraints, with the branch `promises` (from `notes-complete` once Plan B is complete).
- D14: a person exists only because the owner named them; removing a person unlinks their meetings and deletes their briefs. No voiceprints here.
- Names are content: never logged above DEBUG.
- Rail links follow DESIGN.md "Settings in the rail": icon + word (+ count), chipboard ink, hover chip-well, the open page keeps that fill with ink text.

## Review Focus

1. **Two spellings of one person** ("priya", "Priya ") resolve to one person; renaming to a name another person has is refused with a reason. Test: Task 1 `test_names_match_case_and_space`, `test_rename_to_a_taken_name`.
2. **Removing a person** unlinks their meetings (the transcript says "Them" again) and deletes their briefs, nothing else. Test: Task 1 `test_remove_person_unlinks`.
3. **A promise ticked by mistake** can be brought back from the same page within a few seconds (Undo), and from the meeting's note at any time. Test: Task 2 `undo brings a promise back`.
4. **A brief for a person with no notes yet** says so instead of calling the model; a brief whose points all fail the check is not shown. Tests: Task 3 `test_brief_needs_notes`, `test_unchecked_brief_is_not_shown`.
5. **A rewritten note** replaces its meeting's items on the Promises page (no duplicates from the old generation). Test: Task 2 `test_only_current_generation_items`.
